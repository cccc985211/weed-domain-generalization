## GO / NO-GO assessment for the A/B/C trio

- **A (OPBR): CONDITIONAL GO.** Object-preserving background perturbation is prior-art-adjacent (OA-Mix #1, DSD physics-based aug #2). Publishable only as a *sparse-box, margin-tapered* variant with an explicit matched-magnitude control vs standard augmentation and vs an OA-Mix-style mix. No novelty claim until that comparison exists.
- **B (PSAS): WEAK GO.** Scheduling augmentation strength is a small delta over curricula in DG literature; YOLO26's own Progressive Loss (#13) already schedules *loss* emphasis. B must show benefit **controlling for integrated augmentation intensity** and ideally demonstrate orthogonality to Progressive Loss; otherwise fold into A's ablation knob.
- **C (SOFC): GO (primary candidate).** ROI-level, scale-weighted feature consistency across photometric-only dual views is the least-covered idea (#5 #6 #14 are adjacent but differ in level/supervision). On YOLO26 it must be shown complementary to STAL (which fixes *label assignment* for small objects, a different mechanism) — see `model_selection/module_overlap.md`.
- **Overall: GO** for a modules-on-YOLO26 study, *conditioned* on (a) the EXP-002 baseline reproducing sensibly, (b) matched-compute and matched-intensity controls for every module, (c) no claim that the trio is novel as a set — the claim must be per-module and evidence-bound.
